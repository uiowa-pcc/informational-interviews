Informational Interview Planner

Informational interviews are short conversations with professionals or people who have completed internships in fields you want to explore. Use this planner to identify what you want to learn, choose 2–3 possible people to contact, prepare your outreach, and build questions for the conversation.

How might you find someone to talk with?

You do not need to already know the right person. Start by choosing one or more ways you could find someone connected to the career, organization, or field you want to learn about. Then identify 2–3 possible people to contact.

Need help finding someone?

Try searching for people connected to the field rather than searching for the “perfect” person.

  • Use the University of Iowa LinkedIn Alumni Tool. Visit the University of Iowa LinkedIn page, open the Alumni section, and search alumni by where they work, what they do, what they studied, or where they live.
  • Search LinkedIn by job title, organization, or field to find other professionals.
  • Look at staff directories on employer or organization websites.
  • Ask faculty, staff, supervisors, classmates, friends, or family if they know someone you could contact.
  • Explore professional associations related to the field.
  • Ask a Pomerantz Career Center Career Coach to help you brainstorm potential people to contact.
Step 2

Plan your outreach

Edit the message so it sounds like you and explains what you hope to learn. Identify yourself as a University of Iowa student, ask for about 20–30 minutes, and give the person plenty of time to respond and schedule.

Keep the purpose clear: You are asking to learn from their experience—not asking them for a job. Avoid contacting someone the day before and asking to meet the next day.

Create your plan

After the conversation
  • Send a brief thank-you after the conversation.
  • Write down what you learned and any new questions you now have.
  • Consider whether the conversation reinforced your interest and how the career fits you.
  • Keep the person’s contact information for future reference.
  • Follow up on any resources or people they suggested.
